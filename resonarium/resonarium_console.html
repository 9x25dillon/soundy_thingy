Before you begin

Resonarium Console · local-only instrument

Photosensitivity. This page renders slowly animated visuals. All visual modulation is hard-capped at 2.5 Hz, below the 3–30 Hz range associated with photosensitive risk. If you are sensitive to flashing or pulsing imagery, or feel any discomfort, close the page.

Audio. Tones start quiet and all audio is clamped to 20 Hz–18 kHz. Set your system volume low before starting, especially with headphones.

What this is. An aesthetic, generative audiovisual instrument seeded from chart geometry. It is not a medical device and makes no diagnostic, therapeutic, or health claims of any kind.

Privacy. Everything runs in this page. No network requests are made. Chart data and intention text stay in memory only, and are never written to browser storage, the trace, or exports unless you explicitly opt in.

Every parameter in this console carries an mark. Open it for the exact formula, its range, and why the limit exists.
